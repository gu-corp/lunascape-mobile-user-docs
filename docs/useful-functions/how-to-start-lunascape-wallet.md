We provide the web3 wallet called Lunascape Wallet. 
To start the Lunascape Wallet, please follow the steps below:

1.Tap the menu button at the bottom of the page

<img class="hs-image-align-none" height="756" src="https://www.gu-tech.com/hubfs/Top_EN-png-3.png" width="350" loading="lazy" />

2. Select the wallet button
<img alt="Wallet Button" class="hs-image-align-none" height="757" src="https://www.gu-tech.com/hubfs/img-lunascapewallet-en10.png" width="350" loading="lazy" />

3. Tap the create new wallet button
<img alt="Create New Wallet" class="hs-image-align-none" height="757" src="https://www.gu-tech.com/hubfs/img-lunascapewallet-en.png" width="350" loading="lazy" />

4. Create your password and tap the next button

<img alt="Create Password" class="hs-image-align-none" height="841" src="https://www.gu-tech.com/hubfs/img-lunascapewallet-en2.png" width="350" loading="lazy" />

5. If you agree to the term of use, tap the term of use sections and the create account button
<img alt="Terms of Use" class="hs-image-align-none" height="842" src="https://www.gu-tech.com/hubfs/img-lunascapewallet-en3-1.png" width="350" loading="lazy" />

6. Back up your seed phrase as your master key to the wallet and cryptographic funds. Losing the seed phrase means you can't access your crypto on the wallet anymore, so keep it in a safe place. You can check and copy your seed phrase.
<img alt="About Seed Phrase" class="hs-image-align-none" height="844" src="https://www.gu-tech.com/hubfs/img-lunascapewallet-en4.png" width="350" loading="lazy" />

<img alt="Show Seed Phrase" class="hs-image-align-none" height="757" src="https://www.gu-tech.com/hubfs/img-lunascapewallet-en5.png" width="350" loading="lazy" />

<img alt="Export Seed Phrase" class="hs-image-align-none" height="757" src="https://www.gu-tech.com/hubfs/img-lunascapewallet-en6.png" width="350" loading="lazy" />

7. You will successfully land on the My Account page.

<img class="hs-image-align-none" height="716" src="https://www.gu-tech.com/hubfs/%E3%82%B9%E3%82%AF%E3%83%AA%E3%83%BC%E3%83%B3%E3%82%B7%E3%83%A7%E3%83%83%E3%83%88%202024-01-25%203-29-57-png.png" width="350" loading="lazy" />
