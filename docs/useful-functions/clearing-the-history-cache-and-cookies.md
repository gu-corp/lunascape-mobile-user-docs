History, cookies and cache can be cleared in the following way.

1. Tap the menu icon on the bottom

2.Tap "Settings"

<img class="hs-image-align-none" height="749" src="https://www.gu-tech.com/hubfs/Setting_EN-png-Jan-23-2024-10-46-08-7855-PM.png" width="350" loading="lazy" />

3. Tap the category you wish to delete or clear.

<img class="hs-image-align-none" height="809" src="https://www.gu-tech.com/hubfs/%E3%82%B9%E3%82%AF%E3%83%AA%E3%83%BC%E3%83%B3%E3%82%B7%E3%83%A7%E3%83%83%E3%83%88%202024-01-24%207-47-33-png.png" width="350" loading="lazy" />

To Clearing the history
4. Tap "Clear History," then tap "Clear"
