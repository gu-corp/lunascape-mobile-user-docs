## Here are the steps for Picture-in-Picture in iOS

<span>1. Open YouTube and play the video you wish to view</span>

**<img class="hs-image-align-none" height="721" src="https://www.gu-tech.com/hubfs/%E3%82%B9%E3%82%AF%E3%83%AA%E3%83%BC%E3%83%B3%E3%82%B7%E3%83%A7%E3%83%83%E3%83%88%202024-01-24%207-13-13-png.png" width="350" loading="lazy" />

**2. With the video playing, tap the three-point reader in the lower right corner and select "Settings" > "Desktop site"**
<img class="hs-image-align-none" height="705" src="https://www.gu-tech.com/hubfs/%E3%82%B9%E3%82%AF%E3%83%AA%E3%83%BC%E3%83%B3%E3%82%B7%E3%83%A7%E3%83%83%E3%83%88%202024-01-24%207-18-28-png.png" width="350" loading="lazy" />
**

<span>3.  Tap the playing screen to reduce the screen size</span>

<img class="hs-image-align-none" height="731" src="https://www.gu-tech.com/hubfs/%E3%82%B9%E3%82%AF%E3%83%AA%E3%83%BC%E3%83%B3%E3%82%B7%E3%83%A7%E3%83%83%E3%83%88%202024-01-24%207-22-16-png.png" width="350" loading="lazy" />

4.  Picture-in-picture is now complete**
