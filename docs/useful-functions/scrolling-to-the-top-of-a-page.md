Tapping the top of the screen scrolls to the top of the page.

--- On iPhone ---

1.Tap the menu button at the bottom of the page

<img class="hs-image-align-none" height="689" src="https://www.gu-tech.com/hubfs/Top_EN-png-2.png" width="350" loading="lazy" />

2. Tap "Settings" 

<img class="hs-image-align-none" height="703" src="https://www.gu-tech.com/hubfs/Setting_EN-png-2.png" width="350" loading="lazy" />

3. Top"Action"

<img class="hs-image-align-none" height="781" src="https://www.gu-tech.com/hubfs/Tap_Action-png.png" width="350" loading="lazy" />

4.Turn on "Scroll to Top"
