The display rotation can be locked, in the following way.

1.Tap the menu button at the bottom of the page

<img class="hs-image-align-none" height="756" src="https://www.gu-tech.com/hubfs/Top_EN-png-3.png" width="350" loading="lazy" />

2. Tap "Settings" 

<img class="hs-image-align-none" height="773" src="https://www.gu-tech.com/hubfs/Setting_EN-png-3.png" width="350" loading="lazy" />

3. Tap "Action"

<img class="hs-image-align-none" height="782" src="https://www.gu-tech.com/hubfs/Tap_Action-png-1.png" width="350" loading="lazy" />

4. Turn on "Lock Rotation"
