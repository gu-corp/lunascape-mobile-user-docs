# Browsing History

Browsing History lists visited pages by date so you can reopen a page or remove stored history.

## Open a page from history

1. Open **History** from the Home screen or Tool Menu.
2. Find the date and page you want.
3. Tap the entry to open it.

<img src="img/Simulator_Screenshot_-_iPhone_15_-_2025-09-09_at_17.03.36.png" alt="Browsing History screen" width="360" />

## Delete history

- Delete an individual entry from the history list.
- Use **Delete All** to remove all displayed history.
- Use **Settings** > **History** for additional controls such as clearing cookies and cache.

> **Note:** Private Mode pages are not added to browsing history.
