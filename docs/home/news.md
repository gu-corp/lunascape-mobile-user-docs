# News

The News widget shows recent articles from the RSS feeds configured in Lunascape. Use it to open an article directly or move to the full News screen.

## Read an article

1. Find the News widget on the Home screen.
2. Swipe through the available articles or sources.
3. Tap an article to open it in the browser.

<img src="img/Simulator_Screenshot_-_iPhone_14_Pro_-_2025-10-01_at_17.25.28%201.png" alt="News screen" width="560" />

If the widget is empty, check that at least one RSS feed is enabled and refresh the News screen.
