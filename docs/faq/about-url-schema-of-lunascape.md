category: "FAQ"
---

The URL scheme of Lunascape mobile supports Lunascape and iLunascape.

To use it, open another application, such as Safari or Chrome, and launch Lunascape mobile from that application by loading a URL of the form “lunascape://” or “ilunascape://”

For example: you want to open this URL “[https://www.google.com/search?q=yahoo+news+japanese”](https://www.google.com/search?q=yahoo+news+japanese%E2%80%9D) in Lunascape mobile The URL scheme of Lunascape: lunascape://https%3A%2F%[2Fwww.google.com](http://2Fwww.google.com)%2Fsearch%3Fq%3Dyahoo%2Bnews%2Bjapanese
