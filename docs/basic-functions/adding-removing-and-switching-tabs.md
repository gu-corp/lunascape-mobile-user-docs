---

To add a tab:
Tap the "+ button" on the center of the tab bar

* Up to 30 tabs can be displayed at the same time.  (We recommend a maximum of 12 tabs)

<img alt="To add a tab" class="hs-image-align-none" height="834" src="https://www.gu-tech.com/hubfs/img-tab-en.png" width="350" loading="lazy" />

To remove a tab:
Tap the cross button at the bottom right of the screen

<img alt="To remove a tab" class="hs-image-align-none" height="830" src="https://www.gu-tech.com/hubfs/img-tab-en2.png" width="350" loading="lazy" />

To switch tabs:
Slide your finger ("flick") over the tab titles in the Tab Bar.
Alternatively you can switch to another tab by tapping on it.

<img alt="To switch tabs" class="hs-image-align-none" height="757" src="https://www.gu-tech.com/hubfs/img-tab-en3.png" width="350" loading="lazy" />
