To delete a bookmark:
1. Tap the "Bookmarks" button (the book-shaped icon) at the bottom of the screen

<img class="hs-image-align-none" height="1087" src="https://www.gu-tech.com/hubfs/%E3%82%B9%E3%82%AF%E3%83%AA%E3%83%BC%E3%83%B3%E3%82%B7%E3%83%A7%E3%83%83%E3%83%88%202024-01-25%204-01-52-png.png" width="350" loading="lazy" />

2. Tap the "three-point icon"

<img class="hs-image-align-none" height="1143" src="https://www.gu-tech.com/hubfs/%E3%82%B9%E3%82%AF%E3%83%AA%E3%83%BC%E3%83%B3%E3%82%B7%E3%83%A7%E3%83%83%E3%83%88%202024-01-25%204-03-40-png.png" width="350" loading="lazy" />

3. Tap the "Delete" button that appears

<img class="hs-image-align-none" height="726" src="https://www.gu-tech.com/hubfs/%E3%82%B9%E3%82%AF%E3%83%AA%E3%83%BC%E3%83%B3%E3%82%B7%E3%83%A7%E3%83%83%E3%83%88%202024-01-25%204-06-15-png.png" width="350" loading="lazy" />

To edit a bookmark:
1. Tap the "Bookmarks" button (the book-shaped icon) at the bottom of the screen

<img class="hs-image-align-none" height="1087" src="https://www.gu-tech.com/hubfs/%E3%82%B9%E3%82%AF%E3%83%AA%E3%83%BC%E3%83%B3%E3%82%B7%E3%83%A7%E3%83%83%E3%83%88%202024-01-25%204-01-52-png.png" width="350" loading="lazy" />

2. Tap the "Edit" button

<img class="hs-image-align-none" height="726" src="https://www.gu-tech.com/hubfs/%E3%82%B9%E3%82%AF%E3%83%AA%E3%83%BC%E3%83%B3%E3%82%B7%E3%83%A7%E3%83%83%E3%83%88%202024-01-25%204-08-05-png.png" width="350" loading="lazy" />

3. Any bookmark can now be moved or deleted

<img class="hs-image-align-none" height="716" src="https://www.gu-tech.com/hubfs/%E3%82%B9%E3%82%AF%E3%83%AA%E3%83%BC%E3%83%B3%E3%82%B7%E3%83%A7%E3%83%83%E3%83%88%202024-01-25%204-10-06-png.png" width="350" loading="lazy" />

4. Tap a bookmark to change its title, URL, or folder, and save your changes by tapping "Done"
