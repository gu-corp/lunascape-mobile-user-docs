To add a bookmark via the Address Bar:
1. Open the page you would like to your favorites
2. Hold the tab you would like to add to your favorites

<img alt="Hold Tab" class="hs-image-align-none" height="758" src="https://www.gu-tech.com/hubfs/img-addbookmark-en.png" width="350" loading="lazy" />

3. Tap "Add to Favorites"

<img alt="Add to Favorites" class="hs-image-align-none" height="838" src="https://www.gu-tech.com/hubfs/img-addbookmark-en2.png" width="350" loading="lazy" />

To add a bookmark via the Bookmark Menu:
1. Open the page to bookmark

2. Tap the "bookmark button" (book-shaped icon) at the bottom of the screen

<img alt="Bookmark Button" class="hs-image-align-none" height="758" src="https://www.gu-tech.com/hubfs/img-addbookmark-en4.png" width="350" loading="lazy" />

3. In the bookmarks menu that appears, tap "Add Bookmarks" at the bottom

<img alt="img-addbookmark-en5" class="hs-image-align-none" height="824" src="https://www.gu-tech.com/hubfs/img-addbookmark-en5.png" width="350" loading="lazy" />

4. Confirm by tapping "Save"

<img alt="img-addbookmark-en6" class="hs-image-align-none" height="758" src="https://www.gu-tech.com/hubfs/img-addbookmark-en6.png" width="350" loading="lazy" />
