**Refresh a page**
1. Hold a tab you want to refresh.

<img alt="Hold Tab" class="hs-image-align-none" height="757" src="https://www.gu-tech.com/hubfs/img-refresh-en.png" width="350" loading="lazy" />

2. Tap Reload This Tab

<img alt="Reload This Tab" class="hs-image-align-none" height="818" src="https://www.gu-tech.com/hubfs/img-refresh-en-1.png" width="350" loading="lazy" />

**Go to the previous page**
Tap the Back button at the bottom of the screen

<img alt="Back Button" class="hs-image-align-none" height="840" src="https://www.gu-tech.com/hubfs/img-refresh-en3.png" width="350" loading="lazy" />

**Go to the next page**
Tap the Forward button at the bottom of the screen

<img alt="Next Button" class="hs-image-align-none" height="758" src="https://www.gu-tech.com/hubfs/img-refresh-en4.png" width="350" loading="lazy" />
