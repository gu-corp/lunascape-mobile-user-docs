## Before Contacting Support

To help us assist you more effectively, please:

1. **Check our documentation** - Your question might already be answered in our guides
2. **Include relevant details** - Version numbers, error messages, and steps to reproduce
3. **Attach screenshots** - If applicable, include screenshots of any error messages
